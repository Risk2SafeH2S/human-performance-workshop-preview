**"Human error" is where most incident reports stop. It is where the useful questions begin.**

When an operator opens the wrong valve, the retraining and the reminder to "take more care" change very little. The identical valves, the tag facing the pipe rack and the rushed handover are still there for the next person.

Human performance is the discipline of fixing those conditions. It is a skill set, and it can be taught at every level of the organisation.

We have built the **Human Performance Pathway** for oil and gas teams: five assessed levels, from the newest contractor on site to the person who will run your programme.

🔹 **HP-1 Foundation** (1 day): everyone on site
🔹 **HP-2 Practitioner** (2 days): operators, technicians, permit holders, supervisors
🔹 **HP-3 Advanced** (3 days): engineers, HSE and process safety, procedure writers
🔹 **HP-4 Leader** (2 days): superintendents, managers, asset leadership
🔹 **HP-5 Master** (4 days + workplace project): HP leads, lead investigators, in-house facilitators
